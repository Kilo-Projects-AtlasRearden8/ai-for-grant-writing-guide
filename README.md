## The ground rules (read first)

1. **Education only.** This manual teaches how to use AI tools. It is not legal, financial, or medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a funder's own instructions.
2. **Never paste donor data.** Do not enter donor names, contact details, giving history, grant amounts, or any personally identifying information into a free AI tool. See `docs/06-privacy-ethics.md`.
3. **AI drafts, humans decide.** AI can suggest structure and wording. You must review every fact, number, and claim before submitting anything.
4. **Free and local-first.** Every tool mentioned has a free tier, and nothing here requires you to create an account or hand over data.
