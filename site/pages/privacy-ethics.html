This is the most important chapter in the manual. If you remember nothing else, remember this:

Never paste donor data into a free AI tool.

What never goes into AI

  • Donor names, addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses
  • Donor giving history or grant amounts
  • Foundation or corporate funder financial details
  • Client, patient, or student names and identifying details
  • Staff or board member personal information
  • Your organization's confidential budgets, payroll, or tax documents
  • Passwords, API keys, or account credentials

Why

Free AI tools are often trained on or reviewed by humans. Anything you paste could be stored, reviewed, or used to improve the model. Assume nothing you type is private.

What's OK to use AI with

  • Your own program description (no names)
  • General funder research strategies (not specific applications)
  • Drafts you write yourself, with real names removed
  • Your own notes, rewritten in plain language

The human-in-the-loop rule

AI can suggest words. It cannot take responsibility. You verify every fact, number, and date. You answer every funder question yourself. You read the whole application before submitting. You are legally and ethically responsible for what goes out under your name.

Education only

This manual teaches how to use AI tools. It is not legal, financial, or compliance advice. If your organization handles sensitive data, talk to a qualified professional about your data-handling policies.
