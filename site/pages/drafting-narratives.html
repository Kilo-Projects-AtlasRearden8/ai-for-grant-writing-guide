The narrative is the heart of most grant applications: it tells the funder who you are, what problem you're solving, what you'll do, and why it matters. AI is a drafting partner here, not the author. You supply the truth; AI supplies the words.

The golden rule: AI drafts, you verify

AI can hallucinate statistics, invent programs, and sound confident while being wrong. Every fact, number, and claim in your narrative must come from you — and you must check it before submitting.

Sections most narratives ask for

  1. Need statement (the problem)
  2. Goals and objectives
  3. Methods / program description
  4. Evaluation (how you'll know it worked)
  5. Sustainability (what happens after the grant)
  6. Organization background

Step 1: Needs statement

Help me write a "statement of need" for a grant application.
Program: [one-sentence description]
Population served: [who, where]
Problem in plain language: [your rough notes]
Local data I can cite: [any numbers you have, or write "none yet"]

Write 2-3 short paragraphs in plain English. Do not invent statistics.
Mark any place where I still need to find a number with [NEEDS DATA].

Step 2: Goals and objectives

I need 3 goals and 3 measurable objectives for this grant:
Goal: [broad aim]
What success looks like: [your notes]
Time period: [e.g., 12 months]
Number of people served: [your number]

Write each objective so it is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant,
and time-bound (SMART).

Step 3: Program description

Write a "program description" section explaining what the program will do,
who will run it, how often it meets, and where it happens.
Program: [description]
Staff or volunteers: [who]
Schedule: [how often, how long]
Location: [where]

Keep it to 3-4 short paragraphs. Use plain language. Avoid jargon.

Step 4: Match the funder's questions

Read the funder's guidelines first. If the funder asks five specific questions, answer those five — don't let AI write a generic essay.

Here is my draft narrative and the funder's list of questions.
Funder questions: [paste the questions]
Draft: [paste your draft]

Did I answer every question? List anything missing.
Do not rewrite my draft; just tell me what's missing.

Before you submit

  • Every number came from your own records, not AI
  • You answered all of the funder's specific questions
  • Names of real people (clients, donors, board members) were removed or changed
  • A person read the whole thing out loud to catch awkward phrasing

Privacy reminder: Never paste donor data. See Privacy & ethics.
