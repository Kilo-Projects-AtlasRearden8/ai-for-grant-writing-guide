Before you write a grant, you need a funder whose priorities match your program. AI can't browse the internet for you in most free versions, but it can help you think through where to look, what to search for, and how to organize what you find.

What you'll need

  • A clear one-sentence description of your program
  • The population you serve and where they live
  • Roughly how much money you need
  • A notebook or spreadsheet

Step 1: Write your program in one sentence

AI works better when you give it a short, specific description. Don't paste donor data here — this is about your program, not your donors.

Example: "After-school tutoring for middle-school students in rural Jefferson County, focused on reading and math, run by volunteers."

Step 2: Ask AI for funder types to target

I run [one-sentence program description] in [city/county, state].
I need roughly $[amount] for [time period, e.g., one year].
What types of funders should I look for? List:
1. Local and regional foundations
2. Corporate giving programs
3. State or federal grant programs
4. Community foundations or United Way-type funders

For each type, give me 2-3 search terms I can type into a search engine,
and one question I should ask before applying.

Step 3: Search yourself, then let AI organize

Do the actual searching in a search engine or on funder websites. Never paste funder application details, donor lists, or giving histories into a free AI tool.

When you find promising funders, write down: funder name, website, typical grant size, deadlines, what they fund, and how to apply.

Then ask AI to turn your rough notes into a comparison table:

Turn these rough funder notes into a simple comparison table with columns:
Funder | Typical grant | Deadline | What they fund | How to apply | Fit (1-5)

Paste your notes here.

Step 4: Pick 2-3 funders to start

Don't apply to ten at once. Pick two or three that look like a good fit, read their guidelines carefully, and follow their instructions exactly.

Before you submit

  • You read the funder's current guidelines
  • The funder actually funds work in your geography
  • Your request amount is within their typical range
  • You did not paste donor names, contact info, or giving history into any AI tool

Privacy reminder: Never paste donor data. See Privacy & ethics.
