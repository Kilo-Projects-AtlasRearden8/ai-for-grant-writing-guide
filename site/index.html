Welcome

Plain-English, step-by-step help for using free AI to write grants. This manual walks you through finding funders, drafting narratives, building budgets, and writing reports — written like a friend would explain it, never condescending.

Credit: This guide was written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). It is part of the same beginner-friendly AI education line as The Massive AI Manual and dylanasdale.github.io.

Ground rules

  • Education only. Not legal, financial, or medical advice. Always follow the funder's own instructions.
  • Never paste donor data. Donor names, contact details, giving history, or grant amounts never go into a free AI tool.
  • AI drafts, humans decide. Every fact, number, and claim must be checked by you before submitting.
  • Free and local-first. Every tool mentioned has a free tier; nothing here requires an account or handing over data.
