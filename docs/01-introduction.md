# 1. Introduction: AI and grant writing, explained like a friend would

Welcome. If you've never written a grant before — or if you've written one and found it overwhelming — this manual is for you. It walks you through using free AI tools for grant writing, step by step, in plain English.

This guide was written by Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). It follows the same approach as *The Massive AI Manual* and dylanasdale.github.io: warm, unhurried, never condescending, education only.

## What this manual covers

1. Finding funders
2. Drafting narratives
3. Building budgets
4. Writing reports
5. Privacy and ethics (the most important chapter — read it)
6. A printable checklist
7. Fill-in templates you can copy and adapt

## What AI is good at here

- Turning rough notes into complete sentences
- Suggesting a structure for a narrative section
- Rewriting jargon into plain language
- Checking that you've answered every question in the funder's guidelines
- Summarizing your program's results for a report

## What AI is NOT good at here

- Knowing your community the way you do
- Verifying facts, numbers, or dates
- Understanding a funder's unspoken preferences
- Taking responsibility for what you submit

**AI drafts. You decide.** Treat AI like a very fast, very confident intern who occasionally makes things up. Every number, date, name, and claim must be checked by you before it goes anywhere near a funder.

## How to use this manual

Read it in order, or jump to the chapter you need today. Each chapter has:

- A "What you'll need" list
- Step-by-step instructions
- Example prompts you can copy, paste, and edit
- A "Before you submit" check

The templates in `docs/templates/` are fill-in-the-blank starting points. Copy one, fill it out, then use the AI prompts in that chapter to polish it.

## Education-only disclaimer

This manual is for educational purposes. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or medical advice. Funder rules change, and every funder has its own requirements. Always follow the funder's published instructions over anything in this guide.
