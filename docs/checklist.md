# Grant-writing checklist (printable)

Print this page and check items off with a pen.

## Before you start
- [ ] I have the funder's current application guidelines
- [ ] I can describe my program in one sentence
- [ ] I know how much money I'm asking for and why
- [ ] I have a place to keep notes (notebook, spreadsheet, or paper)

## Finding funders
- [ ] I listed 2-3 funders that match my geography and program type
- [ ] I checked each funder's typical grant size against my request
- [ ] I noted each funder's deadline and application method
- [ ] I did NOT paste donor names or giving history into AI

## Drafting the narrative
- [ ] I wrote a needs statement with real, sourced data
- [ ] I wrote SMART goals and objectives
- [ ] I described who runs the program, where, and how often
- [ ] I explained how I'll measure success
- [ ] I answered every question the funder asked
- [ ] I checked every number against my own records
- [ ] No real client, donor, or staff names appear in the draft

## Budget
- [ ] I listed every cost the program needs
- [ ] I got real quotes or estimates where I could
- [ ] I added the amounts myself (spreadsheet or calculator)
- [ ] My budget matches my narrative (no promises without money behind them)
- [ ] I checked what the funder will NOT pay for
- [ ] No donor or funder financial details appear in the budget

## Reports
- [ ] I have my records: people served, activities, outcomes
- [ ] I answered every question on the funder's report form
- [ ] Every number matches my records
- [ ] No identifying details about clients or donors appear
- [ ] Someone else read the report before I submitted

## Before you hit submit
- [ ] I read the funder's guidelines one last time
- [ ] I followed their formatting and submission instructions
- [ ] I saved a copy of everything I submitted
- [ ] I noted the submission date and any reporting deadlines
